# Content Change Log — Removing Fintech/Banking Framing

**Why this pass happened:** the previous pass leaned too hard on banking/fintech language (CBDC, BBPS, "banking-grade", "financial systems", payment-specific icons) because it was drawing directly from one founder's resume. This pass rewrites those sections to read as a general software/tech company that *also* happens to be strong in enterprise engineering and AI automation, rather than a fintech specialist.

Files touched: `index.html`, `about.html`, `feature.html`, `service.html`, `quote.html`. No design/CSS/layout changes — text and a couple of icons only.
